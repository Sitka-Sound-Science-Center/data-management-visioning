# Sitka Sound Science Center – Research Data and Open Science Statement

At the **Sitka Sound Science Center (SSSC)**, we are committed to the principles of **Open Science** to promote **transparency, reproducibility, equity, and collaboration** in scientific research. As a place-based research institution in Southeast Alaska, we recognize our responsibility to manage data in ways that are both **scientifically sound and culturally respectful**.

We use the **Openscapes framework** to guide our work. This includes training, tools, and mindsets that support:
- Reproducible research workflows
- Ethical data stewardship
- Shared documentation and cloud-based collaboration
- Use of open-source software (e.g., R, Python, QGIS)
- Project management with Git and GitHub
- Participatory learning through open communities

---

## Our Commitments

### 1. Reproducibility and Transparency
- We document how data are collected, processed, and analyzed, using structured workflows and version control.
- We apply metadata standards and naming conventions to help others understand and reuse our data.
- We rely on open-source tools and reproducible publishing formats to support research that can be verified and reused.

### 2. Open Access to Data
- We make our data publicly available through secure, persistent repositories, following requirements from our funding partners.
- We do not release sensitive or community-specific information without permission. We honor data agreements and maintain confidentiality where needed.

### 3. Ethical Collaboration and Data Sovereignty
- We work with community and Tribal partners to shape data agreements and research goals.
- We follow the **CARE Principles** (Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, and Ethics) in balance with the **FAIR Principles** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable).

### 4. Scientific Integrity
- We maintain high standards for accuracy, peer accountability, and detailed records.
- We encourage practices such as preregistration, sharing of code and methods, and publication of all results, including those that may not confirm expectations.

### 5. Long-Term Studies
- We support ongoing research efforts that monitor environmental and community change over time.
- We maintain long-term datasets with clear provenance, stewardship plans, and access protocols for future use.

### 6. Public Trust and Transparency
- We are committed to open communication about our data and research.
- We provide summaries of findings in accessible language and formats to support informed decision-making by residents, students, and local leaders.

### 7. Continuous Improvement
- We update our data practices based on community feedback, technical developments, and peer learning.
- Through Openscapes, we participate in national learning cohorts to stay current with modern data strategies.

### Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC)

We define and document three stages of data management:

- **Raw Data** – Collected directly from the field or instruments  
- **Applied Data** – Processed, cleaned, and ready for analysis  
- **Synthetic Data** – Modeled or anonymized for public use  

Each step is guided by documented methods, version history, and ethical review.
